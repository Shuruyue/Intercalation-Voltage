Materials Project insertion electrodes (2019 snapshot) · gradient boosting trained on lithium onlyWhat voltage will this electrode give?

Type the discharged and charged compositions of an insertion reaction. A model trained on lithium reactions predicts the average voltage; a warning light says whether the training data held anything like it.

REACTION

Formulas such as Li3V2(PO4)3 or LiFe0.5Mn0.5PO4; the working ion is the first of Li, Na, K, Mg, Ca, Zn, Al, Y, Rb, Cs found.
